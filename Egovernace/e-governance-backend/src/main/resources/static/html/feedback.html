How Your Feedback Is Handled

We take every submission seriously. Here's a brief overview of our process:

1. Submission

Your feedback is securely submitted and recorded in our system.

2. Review & Categorization

Our dedicated team reviews your submission and assigns it to the relevant department.

3. Action & Resolution

Appropriate actions are taken, whether it's an improvement, investigation, or direct response.

4. Follow-up (Optional)

If contact details were provided, we may follow up with updates on your specific case.
