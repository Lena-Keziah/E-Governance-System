About e-Gov Cameroon

Driving digital transformation for a more connected and efficient Cameroon.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide citizens and businesses of Cameroon with accessible, efficient, and transparent government services through innovative digital platforms, fostering economic growth and social development.

Our Vision

To be the leading force in creating a fully digitized public administration in Cameroon, where every citizen can easily interact with government services, contributing to a modern and prosperous nation.

Our Core Values

Transparency

Operating with openness and accountability in all our processes.

Efficiency

Streamlining services to save time and resources for everyone.

Citizen-Centric

Designing services around the needs and convenience of our users.

Security

Ensuring the highest level of data protection and privacy.

Innovation

Continuously seeking new technologies to improve public services.

Progress

Committed to continuous improvement and national development.
